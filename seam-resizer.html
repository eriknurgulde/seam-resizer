Content-aware resizing you can drag, live.

Drag left to shrink, right to enlarge (up to 180%). Enlarging stretches the plain areas and leaves the objects as they are.

Grows the image on one side only. It stretches the plain parts near that edge, and everything else stays exactly where it was. A bigger fill area lets you add more.

Drag the box to move it, drag the handles to resize it, or drag anywhere outside the box to draw a new one. Any resize or extend you haven't kept yet is kept automatically when you open Crop.

Mark areas

Paint over things that must keep their shape (Protect) or that you want removed first (Remove), then click Done.

Everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded. It uses the same seam-carving idea as Caire. The PNG is saved at the working size; for full-resolution output, use the caire command.
